Technology Sidecar

Someone in the room who speaks technology, on your side.

Upgrading, switching vendors or building out a space? We sit beside you through the sales calls, quotes, installs and support tickets, so the decisions stay yours and the vendors stay accountable.

When to bring us in

The moments vendors count on you not knowing the questions.

  • Choosing a new point-of-sale or payment platform, or leaving one that no longer fits
  • Replacing scanners, receipt and label printers, kitchen screens, headsets or kiosks
  • Changing internet providers, adding a backup connection, or finally fixing the Wi-Fi
  • Opening, moving or renovating a location with electricians and low-voltage contractors
  • A support ticket that is going nowhere while the business waits

What we do beside you

Translate. Coordinate. Escalate. Document.

Translate the offers

We read the quotes and contracts with you: what is included, what is extra, what locks you in, and which questions to ask before you sign.

Coordinate the work

Timelines, dependencies and who does what. We keep vendors, installers and your team moving in the same direction, toward a date you can plan around.

Navigate support

We know how vendor support works from the inside: the tiers, the right words and when to escalate. During an outage we run the incident, so you can run the business.

Leave it documented

Plain-language notes on what you have, who to call and what to do first when something stops working. Simple enough to tape inside the office door.
