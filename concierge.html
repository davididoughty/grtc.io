Technical Concierge · Tech projects done for you in Chester County, PA

Hand us the job. We'll handle the technology.

You know what you want: Wi-Fi in every room, cameras on the doors, a new office wired and working. Tell us the goal and the budget. We scope it, source it, schedule it and tell you when it is done.

How it works

Four steps. You are involved in two.

  1. 01

    Tell us the goal and the budget

    A short conversation or a site visit. What should work, where, and what you want to spend.

  2. 02

    Approve a clear scope

    What is included, what it costs, how long it takes and what you will have at the end. No surprises later.

  3. 03

    We source, schedule and manage

    Equipment, installers and providers are coordinated for you. You get updates, not homework.

  4. 04

    It is done, tested and documented

    Working, verified and handed over with simple notes on what you have and who to call.

Typical requests

If it plugs in, connects or needs a cable, ask.

Wi-Fi everywhere

Every room, the back office, the patio, the detached garage or the barn.

Cameras & doorbells

Doors, driveways and storefronts covered, recorded and viewable on your phone.

Office or store setup

New space or a move: internet, network, printers, point of sale and the desks working on day one.

Smart home

Lighting, voice assistants, thermostats and routines that the whole household can actually use.

Stay-online kits

Battery backup and a backup internet connection, so a storm or outage does not stop the day.

TVs & meeting rooms

Displays, sound and video calling that work the first time someone walks in.
