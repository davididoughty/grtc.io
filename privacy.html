Privacy

Clear expectations. Minimal collection.

A straightforward explanation of what the contact experiences do.

Information you choose to provide

The guided questionnaire assembles your responses in your browser. The website does not store them in a database or local storage. Your answers are transmitted only if you choose a delivery method.

How messages are delivered

If you select Open email app, your device's email software handles the message. If you select Send from website, your name, email address and message are sent to the configured third-party form service, which forwards the inquiry to GRTC. The provider's own privacy and retention practices apply.

Contact and removal requests

For questions about a message you've submitted or requests regarding your information, contact david@grtc.io.

Other services

Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information as part of serving the site. The site does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics in this release.

Review this page against the final hosting, form-provider and analytics configuration before launch. It is a starter disclosure, not legal advice.
