Meet the founder

David Doughty

Founder, Golden Ratio Technical Collective

Technology leadership, translated

Enterprise discipline. Human-scale guidance.

David's career spans technology product ownership, infrastructure, program management, service delivery and operations. He founded GRTC to make that experience useful to homes, local businesses and organizations that want clearer decisions without unnecessary complexity.

His background includes leadership roles with Wendy's, IKEA, Exelon, goPuff and La Salle University—work shaped by reliability, vendor accountability, security, governance and measurable outcomes.

David's approach is practical: understand the environment, explain the tradeoffs, and design the next step in proportion to the need.
