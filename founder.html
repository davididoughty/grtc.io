Meet the founder

David Doughty

Founder, Golden Ratio Technical Collective

Technology leadership, translated

Enterprise discipline. Human-scale guidance.

David's career spans more than two decades in technology, from computer science studies to product ownership, infrastructure, program management, service delivery and operations. He founded GRTC to make that experience useful to homes, local businesses and organizations that want clearer decisions without unnecessary complexity.

His background includes leadership roles with major retailers, restaurant brands, utilities, high-growth startups and higher education—work shaped by reliability, vendor accountability, security, governance and measurable outcomes. Across all of it, one lesson held: real control comes from organization and standardization.

David's approach is practical: understand the environment, explain the tradeoffs, and design the next step in proportion to the need. He translates the technical and plans beside you, in a way you understand.
