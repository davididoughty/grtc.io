About the collective

Small by design. Connected by experience.

Founder: David Doughty. Founder of Golden Ratio Technical Collective.

What collective means

Proportion. Connection. Deliberate growth.

The nautilus is more than our symbol. Its expanding form reflects the principle behind our work: start with a strong center, establish the right proportions, and grow with intention.

Golden Ratio Technical Collective is intentionally focused. The collective is not a claim of a large permanent staff. It describes how we connect technical judgment, vendors, specialists, support ecosystems and practical tools around a specific challenge. We engage outside expertise when appropriate and with your agreement.

Our founder brings experience from complex enterprise environments, where decisions are shaped by reliability, cost, security, governance, vendor accountability and measurable outcomes. We translate those disciplines into clear options and roadmaps, whether you run a business or simply want your home to work better.

You do not need to be technical to work with us. We explain the tradeoffs, define success in terms you can understand, and make the next step visible.

We are not here to sell you the largest solution. We are here to help you reach the right one.

The advantage

Enterprise thinking without enterprise overhead.

Navigate the ecosystem

Vendor selection, technical discovery, support escalation, outside specialists and practical accountability. We know how to frame the questions and coordinate the right people, without claiming partnerships we do not have.

Make the roadmap measurable

Define the current state, identify dependencies and risks, prioritize changes, set meaningful success measures and verify the outcome. Security and resilience are design considerations, not afterthoughts.

Translate complexity

We turn network diagrams, infrastructure choices, operational constraints and technical tradeoffs into decisions a nontechnical owner can make with confidence.

Apply scale appropriately

The discipline behind large technology programs can also guide a small office's Wi-Fi, a storefront's continuity plan or a household's useful automations. The solution should remain proportionate to the need.
