Community survey

Official rules.

The plain-language terms for the GRTC community technology survey and the complimentary assessment.

No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is needed to enter or be selected. Buying GRTC services does not improve your chances.

Sponsor

Golden Ratio Technical Collective LLC (“GRTC”), Chester County, Pennsylvania. Contact: david@grtc.io or (267) 279-9298.

Who can enter

Businesses and organizations with a location in Chester County, Pennsylvania or a neighboring community GRTC serves. The person entering must be 18 or older and an owner, manager or authorized representative of the business. Employees of GRTC and their immediate family members are not eligible. One entry per business.

How to enter

Complete the GRTC community survey at goldenratiotechnicalcollective.com/checkin between 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on December 1, 2026 and 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on Monday, December 21, 2026. Incomplete or late entries are not eligible.

How a business is selected

One business will be selected at random from all eligible entries in the first week of January 2027. The odds of being selected depend on the number of eligible entries received.

What the selected business receives

One complimentary Business Technology Assessment of up to two hours, on-site or remote within the GRTC service area, plus a brief written summary. Approximate retail value: $300. Repairs, equipment, licenses and follow-up work are not included and would be quoted separately if requested. The assessment must be scheduled by March 31, 2027, has no cash value, and cannot be transferred or exchanged.

Notification

The selected business will be contacted by email or phone using the details provided in the survey. If GRTC cannot reach the business, or it does not respond within 7 days, an alternate may be selected at random.

Your information

Survey answers are collected through Google Forms and used to run this selection, understand local technology needs and follow up with you. They are never sold. See the privacy page.

General

GRTC may cancel, modify or suspend this offer if it cannot run as planned for reasons beyond its control. Void where prohibited. By entering, you agree to these rules.
